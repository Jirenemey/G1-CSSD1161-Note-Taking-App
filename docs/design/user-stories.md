# **User Stories**
## **Context**
### Some user stories that will be considered during our app development process.

# **Stories**
- ### **Story 1**: As a note-taking app user, I want to search through my notes quickly.
    - **Given** I have saved the note, **When** I search for my notes, **Then** I want to find the note I am searching for.
    - **Given** I have typed in the search bar, **When** I stop typing, **Then** it shows related notes.

- ### **Story 2**: A student who has a lot of studying to do. And needs a note for what to focus on for the day. 
    -  **Given** I have written a note, **when** I organize by date, **then** I will have all my notes organized.
    - **Given** I have a to-do list, **when** I write my list, **then** I should be able to check off the tasks.
